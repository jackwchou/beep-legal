Beep Terms of Service

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What Beep is

Beep is an invite-only service for small groups of friends. When a member of a group text sends !beep to a thread that includes the Beep number, Beep starts a 3-hour "round": it phones each person in the thread for a short conversation, sends text reminders about the round, and then texts the group a link to an audio roundup made from those calls.

SMS/MMS program terms

  • Program name: Beep
  • How you opt in: Someone you know adds the Beep number to a group text you are in, and a member of that thread texts !beep. Beep then sends a welcome message saying how to opt out. If you don't want to take part, reply STOP.
  • Types of messages: Round start notices, call scheduling and retry messages, reminders to the group, and a link to the finished roundup. Beep does not send marketing messages.
  • Message frequency: Varies. A typical round sends about 3 to 8 messages over 3 hours, and only when someone in your group starts a round.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages and calls from Beep. You'll get one message confirming the opt-out. Reply START to rejoin.
  • Help: Reply HELP for help, or email jackwchou@gmail.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Calls and recordings

Beep calls use an AI voice. Calls are recorded and transcribed so they can be made into the group's roundup. Beep says this at the start of each call. You can hang up at any time, and you can ask for your recordings to be deleted (see the Privacy Policy).

Acceptable use

Only add Beep to group texts made up of people who know each other and expect to take part. Don't use Beep to harass anyone, to send unsolicited messages, or for anything illegal.
