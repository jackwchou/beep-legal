Beep Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What we collect

  • Phone numbers of the people in a group text that includes Beep
  • Messages sent to the Beep number in that thread
  • Recordings and transcripts of Beep calls, and anything you share on them (for example, your name and how you know the group)
  • The audio roundups made from those calls

How we use it

Only to run Beep: starting rounds, placing calls, sending round-related texts, making the roundup for your group, and letting Beep remember context from earlier rounds with the same group.

Who can see it

Roundups are shared only with members of the group they were made for. Service providers that process data on our behalf, solely to operate Beep, are Twilio (calls and messaging), OpenAI (voice, transcription and writing), Supabase (database and audio storage) and Railway (hosting). We do not sell your information.

Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that need it to deliver messages.

Retention and deletion

We keep recordings, transcripts and roundups while your group uses Beep. Reply STOP to stop all contact. To have your data deleted, email jackwchou@gmail.com and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

Beep is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
